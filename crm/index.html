How to read these numbers

Confirmed leads generated
Unique CRM leads created during the selected Dubai calendar month with an evidenced creator source. Records identified as repeat imports of the same known capture origin are counted once.
Lead-to-sale conversion
Unique confirmed cohort leads linked to at least one currently won deal, divided by those same cohort leads. A converted lead is not a sale until a linked deal is won. Multiple won deals from one lead count once in this rate.
Closed deals
Currently won deals whose CRM closing date is in the selected month. Their originating leads can be older. Reopened or lost deals are not counted as won. This is the CRM-recorded closing date, not independently verified payment or contract completion.
Review queue
CRM descriptions or timeline notes can suggest an origin, but do not automatically earn attribution. Check the original enquiry, then correct its source in Bitrix24. This report never updates CRM records. An exact source declaration such as “Instagram @confirmed.handle” is supported.
Historical reports
Past months are cohorts evaluated using current CRM states, not frozen historical snapshots. Deleted records or records hidden by permissions cannot be reconstructed. Totals cover readable CRM records, not all Instagram enquiries.
Not inferred
Contact attempts are not successful conversations. Meeting activities are not proof of attendance. Those outcome metrics remain excluded until the CRM has agreed outcome fields. Customer names, phone numbers, emails and deal values are not requested.
