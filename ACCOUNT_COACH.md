# Kirpa Personal Account Coach

The Social Ranking dashboard derives a personal strategy, complete performance snapshot, evidence-backed recommendations, and recommendation review state for every measured Instagram account.

## Optional declared strategy

An employee record in `handles.json` may include an `accountStrategy` object with `key`, `positioning`, `objective`, `audience`, `approach`, `conversionRoute`, `weeklyCapacity`, `languages`, and `boundaries`.

When it is absent, the dashboard clearly labels the strategy as provisional and infers it from the employee role, strongest measured content pillar, strongest format, current cadence, and practical targets.

## Guardrails

- Existing ranking formulas remain unchanged.
- Incomplete windows produce data-recovery advice, not performance guesses.
- At most three actions are shown.
- Every action includes evidence, execution steps, a success condition, confidence, and a review point.
- The product guarantees a traceable measurement-and-review process, not specific views, followers, leads, or deals.
- Browser-local action tracking is a preview only. Authenticated employee accounts must store actions and return only that employee’s records server-side.
